Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, check-ins, photos, and comments — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does logging a drink need a photo?
A quick camera photo verifies you're actually logging a real drink, keeping the leaderboards honest for everyone.

How is "caffeine in your body" calculated?
It's an estimate based on a standard caffeine elimination half-life, not a personalized medical measurement — see the Body tab in the app for details.

I found a bug, or a drink is missing from the database.
Email us at the address above, or use "Send a suggestion" in the app's Settings — we're actively adding new brands and flavors.
